Usage ring in the composer

A 14px ring left of the model chip shows how much of the member's plan limit its CLI reports used. Hover tells the limit and when it resets; click opens the CLI's own usage figures for that account. Sample data, but every field shown is one the CLI really reports: Claude Code's rate_limit_event and get_usage, Codex's account/rateLimits/read and account/rateLimits/updated, Antigravity's agy -p "/usage" --output-format json and /credits.

1. The ring in the context row

Same place and size in every state; only the arc and its colour change. Neutral while the CLI reports nothing alarming, amber at the CLI's own warning (Claude Code's allowed_warning), red when the CLI refuses requests. The headline is the window closest to its limit.
